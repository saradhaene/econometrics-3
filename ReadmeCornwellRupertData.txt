Panel Data of Individual Wages

Description
A panel of 595 individuals from 1976 to 1982, 7 years of data,
taken from the Panel Study of Income Dynamics (PSID).

total number of observations : 4165 (595 x 7)
observations are for individuals from United States of America

Dataset is known as Cornwell and Rupert Returns to Schooling Data

Variables in the file are
EXP = work experience
WKS = weeks worked
OCC = occupation, 1 if in "blue collar" category
IND = employer, 1 if in manufacturing industry
SOUTH = 1 if individual resides in southern states
SMSA = 1 if individual resides in a city (SMSA)
MS = 1 if married
GENDER = 1 if female
UNION = 1 if wage set by union contract
EDU = years of education
COL = 1 if father of individual is of colour
LWAGE = log of wage = dependent variable in regressions
These data were analyzed in Cornwell, C. and Rupert, P., "Efficient Estimation with Panel
Data: An Empirical Comparison of Instrumental Variable Estimators," Journal of Applied
Econometrics, 3, 1988, pp. 149-155.
